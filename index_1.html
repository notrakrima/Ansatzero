Flagship Project

AI-Driven Solar Panel Recycling

The Sovereign Bottleneck

By 2030, Australia will generate 91,000 tonnes of end-of-life solar panel waste annually, representing over $1 billion in locked material value. Removing high-purity silicon without destroying its purity is a critical bottleneck for the renewable circular economy.

Accelerating Traditional R&D

ISA accelerates discovery by deploying fully autonomous AI architecture. Our predictive models screen thousands of quantum chemical formulations to identify optimal molecular targets capable of cleanly isolating silicon.

Closing the Autonomous Loop

Agentic workflows hand off high-confidence solvent candidates directly to a $2.7M ARC-funded automated robotic laboratory. The robotic platform executes synthesis and feeds data back into the AI engine, actively steering scalable discovery.

Translation to Industry

In collaboration with major renewable developers, we engineer scalable recycling technologies designed for localized deployment within Renewable Energy Zones (REZs), converting a national waste crisis into a sovereign asset.
